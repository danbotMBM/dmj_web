How to play

Each hand you're dealt 3 letter tiles only you can see. Seven shared "community" tiles are revealed in three stages (2, then 2, then 3), with a betting round before and after each reveal — just like Texas Hold'em.

You spell the word. Type a word from your 3 tiles plus the 7 community tiles — the tiles light up as you type, and you can submit and re-submit as the board develops. Your highest-scoring valid word is kept for the hand. Word value is the sum of its letter point values (Scrabble-style). At showdown there's a 20-second window to submit your best word (you can resubmit as many times as you like); highest word wins the pot, ties split it. Submit nothing (or only invalid words) and you score zero.

You start with 1000 chips. Run out and you'll watch from the rail until a seat frees up. Up to 10 players sit at the table; extras wait in a queue.

Redeal — reset your chips

Sets your chip count back to 1000. You can't redeal while you're in a hand.
