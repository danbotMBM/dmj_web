A specific source of tension for me is the need for straigt up human brain, organic recall.
Of course the reality that people offload recall is not new, such as books, dictionaries, search engines, etc., but I belive that AI has a unique set of advantages that allow one not only to offload some recall but also encourage internalized recall.

How can one use the infinite word machine to retain and recall in an age of offloading tought?

Retension and recall inproves the quality of one's life and mind by enabling insights and deeper relationships.
Retension is the back bone of descision making.
Without internalizing information, one cannot understand and navigate the world properly.
Take the retension of history.
How can someone understand the reality, depravity, and dynamics of war without a knowledge of the patterns that humanity falls into?
Learning and ingesting history allows one to interperet the world with its context and prior examples.
This knowledge cannot be offloaded because it reorients one's understanding of the world.
One intrinsically understands and takes in new information based on the foundation of a worldview within one's own mind.
If you hear about some historical fact or anecdote, you will not intensionally reach for your phone and search all the right information to make a judgement.
The backbone of wisdom comes from knowing and understanding the things of the past, without recall, wisdom is meaningless.

In a less academic sense, recall enables deeper relationships through thoughtful moments and intension.
Think about the last time someone asked about your niche interest or topic you brought up the other day.
That warm feeling that makes you feel seen or understood just because someone else thought of you.
Of course, AI does not directly inhibit your ability to be thoughtful.
But I do believe that in a noisy world where recall is a less valued skill, this thoughtfullness decline.
I've personally felt that through business and a low need to internalize facts and figures in every day life, I deprioritized memory as a skill.
And I'm tired of it.
Through memory practice and slowing down, I'm hoping to improve my recall and be more intensional and thoughtful in my relationships.

No doubt, technology, search, and AI reduce the immediate need for recall at times.
For example, searching up a niche technical process at work, can be totally steamlined with AI.
For me, that often looks like a quick search or the right command to accomplish something for debugging and programming.
AI has made getting that simple info way easier than even google had done years ago.
But there is a point, where offloading remembering those commands takes its toll, and I can find myself searching for how to do the same thing multiple times.
A search deja vu of sorts.
At that point, i reflect and see that maybe it was worth just a little more attention to internalize that answer to my query.

So, here are some techniques I have found for using AI for learning and memory more than just offloading my thoughts.

First, AI for personalized, quick, studying.
Recently, I went on a trip to beatiful, arid Morocco.
I desired to learn a bit of the Moroccan language to be able to interact with the people and foster a bit more connection on my trip.
However, Morocco, being broadly influenced by a diverse set of ethnicities over history, speaks a niche dialect of Arabic called Darija.
Its much less common to have on language learning tools due to its nicheness.
Luckily I was able to at the airport ask claude to make me a tourist Darija set of Anki flash cards.
Claude proceeded to make 40 flash cards for me to use in greatings, directions, dining, and shopping.
At first claude game me a text version of these flashcards.
Unfortunately, Anki only imports with a nonstandard file format which you can make on your desktop.
But I asked claude to do it for me instead.
Claude proceeded to convert the text file into the Anki format by just writing a python script and running it in the cloud.
It was so painless adn allowed me to spend like 3 hrs of my flight doing flash cards for Darija.
This lead to some fun interactions with street vendors and friendly Moroccans when I was able to say hello in their native language.
AI allowed me to rapidly make some memory tools that I would not have otherwise.

Secondly, AI for daily details.
As a way to test my recall and increase my general knowledge, I resolved to make a daily trivia game.
I love trivia.
I'm competetive and I always want to learn new stuff.
The tricky part of making a trivia game is you have to know the trivia beforehand, list the questions, categorize them and select their level of difficulty.
AI is great at widely documented aggregate humaan knowledge, typing faster than me, and giving a very fuzzy human like category to thigns.
This set of problems seemed like the perfect applicaiton of AI to me.
You can guide the AI to any topic and go considerably deep before is stops being reliable.
I made a daily game, like wordle, a sharable and approachable game.
It has 3 categories with 3 questions of increasing difficulty.
So, every day ther is a little set of 9 trivia questions on a variety of categories to perk your curiosity and test your knowledge.
I hope you enjoy playing it and learn a thing or two.

In a world where knowledge is just one click away, its hard to feel like it needs to be closer.
But AI equips us with even more tools to educate and improve our own minds, if we choose to use them so.
